ERP · Release 1

Review & testing — the paperless quote

▶️ End-to-End Journey — click through the whole lifecycle Walk one renovation from first enquiry to final profit across all 12 stages (lead, site visit, estimate, quote, acceptance, project, purchasing, execution, invoicing, collections, supplier payments, close). A live ledger fills in committed vs actual cost, revenue, margin, cash and forecast as you advance — the fastest way to test the full flow. 📊 Control Tower — customer journey dashboard The whole business at a glance: the 12-stage customer journey (lead → profit), per-project financial control (budget · committed · actual · revenue · margin · cash · forecast), the full dashboard suite (pipeline, receivables, payables, cash forecast, supplier comparison, company performance), auto-drafted emails you send with one click, and alerts & tasks by owner. 🏗️ Frontend — interactive demo Catalogue-first quote builder (18 chapters, measurements), optionals shown separately, acceptance with option selection, an invoice whose VAT is decided by a legal rule (art. 91.Uno.2.10º) with a chained SHA-256 seal, plus the supplier comparison. Same business logic as the real system. 🗄️ Backend — API with real responses The backend endpoints (tenant composition, quotes, invoices with persisted fiscal justification, health) with responses captured from the real server during build verification.
Working language is English; the fiscal invoice renders in es-ES (Spanish VAT). All data is synthetic (no real customer data). The deployable backend with a database arrives with the hosting decision (delivery phase R4); until then it runs from the repository with pnpm dev.
