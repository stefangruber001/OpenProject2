What's in this guide

Work through it in order — later sections reuse what earlier ones set up. Sections marked required block invoicing until they are done; everything else can be finished in the first week.

The one-sentence version. Everything the ERP prints — a quote, a contract, an invoice, a reminder — is assembled from one place: Configuración → Empresa. Fill that in properly and nothing downstream has to be typed twice; leave it empty and the system will politely refuse to issue a single invoice, which is the correct behaviour and not a fault.

How this guide relates to the other one

Two documents, and they do not overlap:

  • This guide — Initial Company Setup. The one-off work: identity, tax defaults, catalogue, people, bank. Done once, revisited when something about the company changes.
  • Operations Guide. The everyday work: lead → visit → quote → contract → project → purchasing → hours → invoice → collection → close. Read it after this one.

Both open from the 👤 profile menu in the top-right corner of the workspace.

Decisions to make (nobody can make them for you)

Tax

Your standard IVA and IRPF

Most renovation work for private customers runs at the reduced rate; new build and commercial work does not. Set the one you use most often — each quote and invoice can still be changed line by line.

Terms

How long you give customers to pay

The number you put in Plazo de pago is what every due date and every overdue alert is computed from. Put in what you actually enforce, not what you wish.

Language

Spanish, Catalan or English

The workspace language is per person; the document language follows the customer's own preference, so a Catalan client can receive a Catalan quote while you work in Spanish.

People

Who gets a login at all

A worker whose hours somebody else enters does not need an account. Give logins to the people who will type — see the roles table in section 8.

You do not have to finish in one sitting. Everything saves as you type it. The only sequence that matters is that the company record (section 3) comes before your first quote, and the catalogue (section 7) makes quoting fast rather than possible.

Section 2

Getting in: the app and the browser

The same system, two ways in. Both show the same records — there is no synchronising, no "phone copy" and nothing to reconcile.

On a computer

A web address

Open the address your installer gave you in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Nothing to install. Bookmark it — the office will live here.

On a phone or tablet

The Canei Subirats app

Installed from TestFlight while the app is in beta, and from the App Store afterwards. It is the same workspace in a native shell, with the camera wired in for site photos.

The first sign-in

1
You receive an invitation

Whoever installed the system creates the first administrator — you. The invitation carries a personal activation link.

2
Open the link and set a password

The link is single-use and belongs to one email address. If it has expired, ask for the invitation to be sent again; nothing is lost.

3
Choose your language

Top-right, in the header. Spanish, Catalan or English — your choice only, it does not change what your colleagues see or what your customers receive.

Finding your way around on a phone

The screen is small, so the layout is not the same as on a computer, and two things are worth knowing on day one:

  • The bottom bar carries five section icons. Tapping the section you are already in opens the list of its screens.
  • Configuración — where everything in this guide happens — is not in that bar. It is the ⚙️ button in the header, to the left of your profile picture. On a computer it is the last icon in the left-hand rail instead.
Set the company up on a computer if you can. Everything here is possible on the phone, but you are typing thirty fields, an address and a bank account. The phone is built for the site: photos, hours, progress, a quick look at the numbers.

Section 3 · required

Your company record

Configuración → EmpresaORG-01

This is the single most important screen in the system. Everything printed on a quote, a contract, an invoice or a reminder is read from here, so it is typed once and never again.

The four fields that unlock invoicing

Spanish invoicing law (RD 1619/2012) requires an invoice to say who issued it, its tax number and where it is; and an invoice nobody can pay is not an invoice. So the system refuses to issue anything until these four are filled:

FieldWhat to putCard
Denominación socialThe full legal name exactly as it appears on the deed — including S.L., S.L.U. or S.A.Identidad
NIF / CIFNine characters, no spaces or dashes: B12345674Identidad
Dirección de trabajoThe address that goes in the document header — your office, which may not be the registered oneDirección de trabajo
IBANThe account customers transfer to. It is printed on every invoiceBanco

Until all four are present, a red band sits at the top of the Control Tower and of this screen naming exactly what is missing. When they are there the band turns green and reads "La empresa puede emitir presupuestos, contratos y facturas."

Identidad

  • Nombre comercial — the name customers know you by, if it differs from the legal one. Used in headings and emails; the legal name is still what appears in the fiscal block.
  • Logotipo — upload it once. It is embedded in every PDF and in the header of every generated email, so a change here restyles the whole document set.

Two addresses, on purpose

Domicilio social is the address in the Registro Mercantil; it belongs in the fiscal block at the foot of a document. Dirección de trabajo is where you actually are and what goes in the header. For most companies they are the same — tick «Igual que el domicilio social» and the second one fills itself and stays in step.

Registro Mercantil

Registry, tomo, folio and hoja, from the deed. These print in small type at the foot of your documents. They are not required to invoice, but a document without them looks like it came from a company that has not registered.

Contacto and Pie legal

Phone, email and website appear in the document header. Pie legal is a free line printed at the foot of every document — data-protection wording or a reference to your general conditions. Ask your gestoría what they want it to say; leave it empty until they answer rather than inventing it.

Changing it later

You can edit this screen at any time, and you should when the company moves or changes bank. Two things to understand about what that does:

  • Documents already issued do not change. An invoice is a legal record of what was sent; it keeps the identity, the address and the account it was issued with.
  • New documents pick the change up immediately. There is nothing to regenerate and no cache to clear.

Section 4

The numbers behind every document

Configuración → Empresa → Valores por defectoORG-01

Nine numbers that used to be fixed inside the program and are now yours. They are applied to new documents; a quote or contract that already exists keeps the values it was created with.

SettingWhat it decidesTypical
IVA por defectoThe tax rate proposed on each new quote and invoice line. Changeable per line — this is the starting point, not a rule21 % / 10 %
IRPF por defectoWithholding proposed where it applies. Leave at 0 if you do not withhold0 % / 15 %
Plazo de pagoDays from issue to due date. Drives every due date, the ageing of receivables and the overdue alerts30 days
Validez del presupuestoHow long a quote stands. Printed on the quote and used to mark it expired30 days
Interés de demoraAnnual late-payment interest quoted in your conditions8 %
Días de cortesíaGrace after the due date before an invoice is treated as overdue and starts appearing in alerts5 days
Planificar enHow soon after acceptance a job should be scheduled before the system flags it7 days
Empezar enHow soon after scheduling work should actually start before it is flagged15 days
Margen mínimo, alertaThe margin below which a quote or a job raises a warning. The number that stops you selling work at a loss15 %
The last one earns its keep. «Margen mínimo» is the only setting on this page that catches a mistake before it costs money. Set it to the margin you would refuse to work below, not to the one you dream of — an alert that fires on every job is an alert nobody reads.

A note on how rates are stored

You type 21 and the system stores 2100 — basis points — because 21 % of one cent has to land somewhere predictable. You will never see that; it matters only if you ever export the raw data.

Section 5

The company mailbox

Configuración → Empresa → Correo de la empresaORG-01

Connect the address your company already sends from — the shared one, not a personal account. Everything the system writes for you ends up in its Drafts folder.

What connecting it actually does

It does

Write finished drafts

Quote emails, invoices, payment reminders, the accountant's package, user invitations — each one composed, branded, with the PDF attached, and filed in Drafts.

It does not

Send anything, ever

There is no send button in the system and no mail server behind it. You open the draft in your own mail program, read it, and press send. Nothing leaves your company without a person deciding it should.

Connecting it

1
Type the address and its password

Use the mailbox everybody already recognises — info@, administracion@ — so replies land where somebody reads them.

2
Press «Conectar el buzón»

The system connects to the mail server for real before saving anything. A wrong password fails here, on this screen, rather than silently three weeks later.

3
Check your Drafts folder

Generate any document and look. If the draft is there, the connection is done.

Where the password lives. Encrypted, on the server, and nowhere else. It is not part of the company data, so it does not travel to phones, does not appear in exports or backups of your records, and cannot be read back from this screen by anyone — including you. To change it, type a new one.
If you skip this section the system still works: every document is produced and downloadable, and you attach it to an email yourself. You lose the branded body text and the filing, not the document.

Section 6

Accounts, cards and cash

Configuración → Empresa → Cuentas y tarjetasORG-01

Every euro that moves belongs to an account. Create one row for each real place money sits — and none for places it does not.

KindCreate one for…
Bank accountEach current account, with its IBAN. The one on your invoices is normally the first
Credit cardEach card. A card is its own account, not a line inside the bank account, because its statement arrives separately and is reconciled separately
Cash boxThe petty cash the site actually uses — the float that pays for a bag of screws and the coffee

Retiring an account

Two different buttons, because they answer two different questions:

  • Desactivar — the account stops appearing in the lists you choose from, and everything already reconciled against it stays exactly as it is. This is what you want for a closed account.
  • Borrar — removes it entirely. If it has movements behind it the system tells you how many and that they go with it. If you are not certain, deactivate.
Why this is on the company screen and not on the banking screen. Accounts belong to the company, not to the act of reconciling. They used to be created inside bank reconciliation, which meant a workspace that had been demonstrated on carried its demonstration accounts for ever, with nowhere to remove them.

What happens next with these

Once accounts exist you can import a bank statement (Administración → Consolidación bancaria), match movements against invoices and bills, and give cash spending on site a destination. None of that is setup — it is in the Operations Guide — but it is why this section matters now.

Section 7

Your catalogue of work

Configuración → Partidas y subpartidasDMC-01 · Unidades de medidaDMC-03

The catalogue is what makes a quote take fifteen minutes instead of two hours, and it is the reason costs can later be compared against what you sold. It is worth an evening.

Two levels, and what each is for

Level 1

Partida — the chapter

The blocks a customer recognises: demolition, plumbing, electrics, tiling, carpentry, painting. This is how the quote is grouped and how the job's money is reported back to you.

Level 2

Subpartida — the line

The thing you actually price: "chapado de pared, 60×60, porcelánico". Each carries its unit, its price and, where it helps, brand, model and quality.

Building it from what you already have

1
Take your last three quotes

Not a blank sheet. The chapters you already use are the right ones, and they are already in your customers' vocabulary.

2
Enter the chapters first, all of them

Ten to fifteen is normal for a reformas company. Get the list right before you go down a level.

3
Then the sub-chapters you use every week

Not every line you have ever quoted. The rare ones can be typed straight onto a quote as a one-off, and added to the catalogue if they turn out to repeat.

4
Check the units of measure

m², ml, ud, h, kg, jornada. The unit is what makes a measurement mean something — "18" is a number, "18 m²" is work.

Prices are a starting point, not a promise. Every price can be overridden on the quote, and the quote remembers what it was sold at. Put in the price you would normally quote today and correct it as you go; a catalogue that is 80 % right on Monday beats one that is perfect in March.
This is also your cost reporting. Purchases, subcontractor bills and hours are all charged to a chapter — and, where you want the detail, to a sub-chapter. A catalogue with three chapters gives you a job report with three lines. That is the whole trade-off, and it is worth ten more minutes now.

Section 8

Your people and their access

Datos maestros → Personal internoDMT-04 · Configuración → UsuariosDMC-08

Two separate ideas, and confusing them is the commonest setup mistake: a worker is somebody whose hours and cost the company records. A user is somebody who can log in. Many people are both. Some are only one.

First: the workers

In Personal interno, one record per person on the payroll, with their hourly cost and overtime rate. This is what turns hours into money: every hour booked to a job is valued at that person's cost and lands on the job's chapter.

Cost, not price. This is what the person costs you per hour — gross plus social security, as your gestoría computes it. It never appears on a customer document; it appears in your job margin, which is the number that tells you whether the work was worth doing.

Then: who can log in

In Usuarios, invite people by email address and give each one a permission level:

PermissionCan do
AdministradorEverything, including managing users. The owner, and normally one other person so you are never locked out
OficinaQuotes, invoices, collections and payments. Does not manage users
Trabajador de obraBooks their own hours and sees only their own. No amounts, no customers, no rest of the ERP
GestoríaRead and export only. Never sees margins or selling prices
«Trabajador de obra» is a boundary, not a label. The screens a site worker cannot use are not merely hidden from them — the server refuses the request as well. A worker cannot reach another worker's hours, or the money, by typing a different address into the phone.

Section 8 · continued

Inviting somebody, and linking them to their hours

What happens when you invite

1
You enter an email and a permission level

The account is created immediately, in state Invitado, with a temporary password that works straight away.

2
The invitation is written into your Drafts

A proper branded email carrying the sign-in address and that password. If the company mailbox is connected (section 5), the drawer confirms «Borrador en el buzón de la empresa» — open it in your mail program and press send.

3
Or hand it over directly

The drawer has Copiar todo: the address, the user and the password in one block, ready to paste into WhatsApp — which is what site crews actually read.

4
They sign in and the account turns Activo

Signing in is what activation means; until it happens the account stays listed as invited, so you can always see who has not got in yet.

The temporary password is temporary. You know it, because you just read it off the screen — so ask the person to change it once they are in. The invitation also carries a one-time link that lets them choose their own password instead, valid for 7 days; either route works and neither is required.
If the drawer warns that the link cannot be reached from outside, the server does not yet know its own public address. The account and the password are valid; the address in the email is not. Tell whoever installed the system — it is one setting on their side — rather than inviting the person again.

Linking a login to a worker record

A site worker's account is joined to their Personal interno record. That link is what makes «Mis horas» show their hours: without it the person can log in and see an empty sheet, which looks exactly like a broken app. When you invite a site worker, set the link at the same time.

Section 9

The small lists

Four short screens that shape the drop-downs you will use every day. Ten minutes between them, and they are the difference between reporting you trust and a "varios" bucket.

Fuentes de leads DMC-04

Where enquiries come from: referral, own website, property manager, lead platform, passing trade. Every opportunity is tagged with one, so this list is what later answers "which of these is actually worth paying for". Keep it short — six entries you will use beats twenty you will not.

Formas de pago DMC-05

Transfer, direct debit, card, cash, and any staged arrangement you offer. Chosen on the quote, carried to the contract, printed on the invoice, and used when matching bank movements.

Comunicaciones DMC-06

The wording of what goes out: the covering text for a quote, an invoice, a payment reminder, a review request. The defaults are written and usable — read them once in your own voice and adjust the two or three that do not sound like you.

Idioma DMC-09

Spanish, Catalan or English. Two independent choices:

  • Your workspace — what you see. Personal, changed from the header at any time.
  • The customer's documents — set on the customer's own record. A Catalan customer receives a Catalan quote whatever language the office works in.
Unidades de medida lives here too (DMC-03), but it belongs with the catalogue — see section 7.

Section 11

When something looks wrong

The five things new companies hit, and what each one actually means.

What you seeWhat it means, and what to do
A red band: «Sin estos datos no se puede emitir ninguna factura» One of the four required fields is empty; the band names which. This is the system protecting you from issuing an invoice that is not valid. Fill it in — section 3.
The demonstration data is still there The workspace ships with two years of example records so you can look around. The profile menu has ↻ Recargar datos de demostración; on a live company register the reload is refused on purpose. Ask your installer for a clean register rather than deleting records one by one.
A site worker signs in and sees no hours Their login is not linked to a worker record. Configuración → Usuarios, edit the person, set the link — section 8.
Somebody cannot sign in with the password you sent Issuing a new invitation replaces the old temporary password, so an older email no longer works. Re-issue from Configuración → Usuarios and send the newest one — section 8.
Nothing is arriving by email Correct. The system never sends. Look in the Drafts folder of the company mailbox — section 5.

Your data is yours

The profile menu carries ⤓ Exportar datos: a complete copy of your records, at any time, without asking anybody. Take one when the setup in this guide is finished — it is a good day to have a copy of.

What to read next

The Operations Guide, from the same profile menu. It picks up exactly where this one stops: the first lead, the site visit, the quote, and the whole chain through to a paid invoice and a five-star review.

One last thing worth knowing. Adding a second company, or changing what the tax rules do, is configuration here — not re-programming. Legal rules carry the date they apply from, and the reason a document was computed the way it was is stored on the document itself.
