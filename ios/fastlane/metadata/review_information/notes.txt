THIS IS A CUSTOM APP, distributed privately through Apple Business Manager to a
single organisation: Canei Subirats, S.L., a renovation contractor in Sant Just
Desvern, Barcelona. It is that company's own management system. It is not
intended for the general public, it is not listed on the App Store, and it has
no public sign-up — accounts are issued by the company's own administrator,
which is why demo credentials are provided above.

WHAT THE APP IS
A native shell around the company's web workspace, served over HTTPS from the
company's own server in the European Union. Staff use it to run renovation jobs
end to end: enquiry, site visit, quote, contract, the work itself, purchases,
hours worked, invoicing and collection.

WHAT TO LOOK AT AFTER SIGNING IN
1. Torre de control — the owner's dashboard: pipeline, cash, margins, alerts.
2. Proyectos > Recorrido del cliente — pick any job; thirteen stages show where
   it stands, and each stage carries the real customer document.
3. Proyectos > Obras — open a job for its Gantt chart and physical progress.
4. Administración > Horas — the site/office split: a day sheet for the crew, a
   review step for the office, and a summary.

LANGUAGE
The interface opens in Spanish. To read it in English: the profile button in the
top-right corner (the circle with initials) > Idioma > EN.

DATA AND PRIVACY
No data is stored on the device beyond the session cookie and an
interface-language preference. The camera is used only when the operator
attaches a photo to a site visit, and the photo goes to the company's own
server. No advertising, no analytics, no third-party trackers, no IDFA.
Privacy policy: https://178-105-10-156.sslip.io/privacy.html

The app requires a network connection; there is no offline mode.
