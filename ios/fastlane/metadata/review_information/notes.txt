Canei Subirats is the private management system of one renovation company
(Canei Subirats, S.L., Sant Just Desvern, Barcelona). It is not a consumer app
and it has no public sign-up: accounts are issued by the company's own
administrator, which is why demo credentials are provided above.

WHAT TO LOOK AT AFTER SIGNING IN
1. Torre de control — the owner's dashboard: pipeline, cash, margins, alerts.
2. Proyectos › Recorrido del cliente — pick any job; the thirteen stages show
   where it stands, each stage carries the real customer document.
3. Proyectos › Obras — open a job for its Gantt chart and physical progress.
4. Administración › Horas — the site/office split: a day sheet, a review step
   and a summary.

LANGUAGE
The interface opens in Spanish. To read it in English: the profile button in
the top-right corner (the circle with initials) → Idioma → EN.

DATA AND PRIVACY
The app is a shell around the company's own web workspace, served over HTTPS
from the company's server in the EU. It stores no data on the device beyond the
session cookie and an interface-language preference. The camera is used only
when the operator attaches a photo to a site visit, and the photo goes to the
company's own server. No advertising, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
Privacy policy: https://178-105-10-156.sslip.io/privacy.html

The app requires a network connection; there is no offline mode.
