Canei Subirats runs a renovation business from the first enquiry to the paid invoice — on the phone, on site, and in the office.

ONE THREAD, NOT SEVEN TOOLS
A customer calls. You book the visit, take the photos, build the quote from your own catalogue, send it, and when it comes back accepted the job opens by itself with the budget frozen behind it. Purchases, hours and supplier bills land against that budget as the work happens, so the margin you see is the margin you have — not the one you hoped for in March.

THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY, IN THIRTEEN STAGES
Every job shows where it actually stands: quote sent, contract signed, work started, invoiced, collected, closed. Each stage carries the real document — the actual quote or invoice, not a link to one — and tells you the single thing that moves it forward. A full history of what has happened sits underneath, with the paperwork attached.

HOURS, WHERE THEY HAPPEN
The crew books the day's hours on the phone against the job and the chapter. The office reviews the week, corrects what needs correcting and approves it. Every hour is valued at that person's cost and charged to the right part of the job, so labour stops being the cost nobody can see. Cash paid on site is reconciled with the month.

BUILT FOR A SPANISH RENOVATION COMPANY
Quotes, contracts and invoices in Spanish, Catalan or English — the customer's own language, chosen per customer. Invoices numbered in sequence, sealed and gap-free. Tax handled by dated rules, with the reason stored on the document itself.

WHAT ELSE IS IN IT
• Control tower: pipeline, cash, margins and what needs attention today
• Quotes built from your catalogue of chapters and line items
• Variations as addenda — the document prints the difference, not the whole job again
• Gantt chart with physical progress and the money that follows it
• Purchase orders and supplier bills booked against the job
• Bank statement import and reconciliation, petty cash included
• A package for your accountant, and every document as PDF, Word or Excel

Emails are never sent behind your back. The app writes the message, attaches the document and leaves it in your own Drafts folder — you read it and press send.

An account is required. Canei Subirats is the private system of one company; access is issued by that company's administrator.
