## One page — the decision

**The thesis.** A factory that emits a jurisdiction-compliant ERP from a specification is worth more than any single ERP it emits, and the province of Barcelona holds roughly 7,000 renovation and finishing contractors (ESTIMATE) who are small enough to serve as configuration and numerous enough to repeat — so the asset to sell is the separation of Spanish invoicing law from the reusable capabilities, proven from both sides by a linter and a negative test, and the thing to prove in the next ninety days is not demand but **delivery**: that one real firm can be onboarded onto the shipping product, on its own bank, inside the measured cost.

**The seven Tier-A design partners**, all S.L., all registry-verifiable, none gate-cleared: **Gonzvilarsa S.L.** (Sant Joan Despí, CNAE 4399 — Segment 1, finishing trades); **A Barcelona Reformas SL** (Barcelona, 4399 — S1); **Reformas Y Construcciones Montcada SL** (Montcada i Reixac, 4399 — S1); **Instalaciones Y Reformas De Calidad SL** (Badalona, 4399 — S1); **Edificaciones Y Reformas Mollet 2000 SL** (Mollet del Vallès, 4121 — Segment 2, renovation-led general contractors); **Delgado & Dilla Construccions I Reformes Integrals SL** (Mollet del Vallès, 4101 — S2); **Reformas Lluca Les Corts SL** (Sabadell, 4101 — S2). Four of the seven have no usable contact channel in the data we hold.

**Phase 7's verdict, in its own words, because it governs the ask.** The fifteen-minute Definition of Done is true of the **specification** and false of the **tenant**: four commands and 3.74 seconds of machine time produce a validated spec, but a signed-in, branded, data-loaded, smoke-tested tenant measured **8 hours (S3), 11 (S1), 16 (S2)** — 32 to 64 times the target — and **two customers cannot yet safely share one stack**, because a tenant is baked into the container image and the session token carries no tenant claim. Those two structural blockers, at **5–10 weeks**, are the first engineering ask; everything commercial waits behind them.

**The ask: €55,000 and 13 weeks** (range €45,000–€75,000). Composition: the three Phase-11 experiments €9,320; paid enrichment and re-extraction €2,000; the N43 bank-statement adapter €3,500; four cheap L0 correctness fixes €1,120; **the two structural blockers €19,600–€39,200 (5–10 weeks)**; first tranche of the Verifactu submission build €8,400 (ESTIMATE, uncosted anywhere before this document); infrastructure €600.

**The red team's verdict is "Proceed with conditions"**, all of them gating. It also writes the stop rule: "Fail experiment 1 and stop."

## Market

The market is defined by a job, not a category: _let me know, while a job is still running, whether I am making money on it — and let the paperwork that proves it to my customer, my subcontractors, my crew and my accountant come out of the same record, in the language each of them reads, without anyone typing it twice._ In is project-based trade work where the quote is the contract, where margin is decided at purchase time, inside the Spanish tax perimeter, with subcontractors and a real split between an operational and an administrative decision-maker. Out is property development (no quote-to-acceptance spine), civil engineering and public work (a filing and registry universe the jurisdiction pack does not hold), recurring-maintenance businesses (no contract-and-service-level object exists), sole traders, and firms above roughly fifty employees.

Sizing, with the labels Phase 1 attached and which this document carries rather than drops. Sourced: 389,146 Spanish construction enterprises at 1 January 2025 (INE DIRCE), 68,134 in Catalonia, 145,189 with at least one employee, 95% under ten employees employing 44.9% of personnel, 5% of firms taking 62% of turnover. Everything provincial is an **ESTIMATE** by apportionment, because no provincial construction count at three-digit CNAE by employee band was retrieved — so **SAM is an ESTIMATE**: roughly **7,000 firms and about €30 million a year in Barcelona province**, 9,500 and €40 million in Catalonia. SOM is set by supply: 40 / 75 / 120 tenants at month 36, €0.19m–€0.58m of annual recurring value, **ESTIMATE throughout**.

Two things matter more than those numbers. At central case this market supports a small team, not a venture outcome, unless the price or the geography expands — the same arithmetic applied to Spain multiplies the count by about 5.7 with no new jurisdiction work. And, conceded to the red team without qualification: **halving or doubling the SAM changes no decision here**, because the binding constraint is two concurrent pilots and five to ten weeks of platform work. The sizing is context, not a lever.

The adjacency map ranks interior fit-out at 95/100 (L0, the reference trade relabelled) and restoration at 88 (L0–L1) — the two to pursue, at no extra sourcing cost and in the same buying centre — then project installers 78 (L0 for project work, L2 for service work, 14,357 Catalan firms), solar retrofit 70, landscaping 58, facility management 44, industrial maintenance 38.

## Ideal customer and scoring

The profile: a Spanish S.L. of 8 to 40 employees and €1.0m–€8.0m in CNAE 43.3, 43.9 or the renovation end of 41.2, within sixty minutes of Sant Just Desvern, winning work by chaptered priced quotation, running four to twenty jobs of €20k–€400k at once, passing at least a third of cost through third parties whose prices it compares by hand, with separate operations and administration owners, a quarterly gestoría, Spanish and Catalan, and the margin question living in one person's head. Six hard gates decline regardless of score: any L5 demand, outside the Spanish tax perimeter, material public-sector revenue, a live incumbent ledger, majority recurring-maintenance revenue, and refusal of the processor agreement or the pre-installation baseline.

The instrument is a CAGE adaptation whose origin is the reference implementation itself, weighting layer distance 40, administrative, economic and cultural 16 each, geographic 12. The finding that governs what this list is worth: **every score is capped at 63.6 out of 100 — the "Qualified" band, when "Ideal" begins at 85 — because three of the five weighted dimensions are honestly unknown for all 127 scorable rows and were scored 5/10 flat and flagged PROVISIONAL**, exactly as the rubric's own unknown rule requires. Confidence is never reported as High, for the same reason.

The data behind that cap is worse than thin. Of twenty-two extracted fields four survive QA — `legal_name`, `trade_name`, `nif`, `cnae` — and two of those rest on fewer than five verified observations. **`municipality` was 30.8% wrong in the 10% sample**, across four independent failure mechanisms, and is excluded from scoring outright; `province` failed at 7.7%; `nif` and `cnae` are null in 64.1% of 128 rows; `directors[]`, `permits_last_24m`, `public_contracts_count`, `permit_value_total_eur`, `review_avg`, `review_recency_months` and `known_software[]` are empty in 128 of 128; one row was a real company in Gondomar, Pontevedra. **The list requires paid enrichment and a re-extraction before any outreach**, and the cheapest action in the programme is recovering one field — CNAE for the 64 Tier D rows declined only for its absence, which would likely move most of them into Tier B.

Tiers, on that basis: **Tier A seven firms**, selected out of a 31-way tie at 63.6 on whether a NIF and a contact channel were recovered and on geographic spread — not on confirmed fit. **Tier B thirty.** **Tier C thirteen** across three packs, two declined on arithmetic. **Tier D seventy-eight**, sixty-four of them declined for a missing field rather than anything about the business, and **zero for an L5 reason**, because L5 evidence only appears in a sales conversation and none has happened.

## Value case

Three segments fall out of the thirty-seven Tier A+B firms on the only two fields QA certifies. **S1, finishing trades** (CNAE 43.3x/43.9x, S.L., twelve firms, four of Tier A): three failures modelled at €23,550 of captured annual value — uncompared purchasing, unproved scope and absorbed extras, the administrative quarter — against €300 a month, 6.5:1, payback month six, breaching twenty-four months only if captured value falls to 24% of central. **S2, renovation-led general contractors** (CNAE division 41, S.L., nineteen firms, three of Tier A): €63,040 captured against €60 per active site per month floored at €400 and capped at €900, 9.7:1, payback month five, breach at 16% of central. **S3, owner-partner micro-contractors** (non-S.L., six firms, none in Tier A): €10,200 against €180 a month, 4.7:1, payback month eight — **conceded in part**, because its 37%-of-central breach threshold is reachable on two individually plausible assumptions.

Every one of those euro figures is built from a physical quantity, a price and a capture rate, and each is labelled an **assumption with a range**. Three carry the document: the 1.5% overpayment on third-party spend (range 0.5%–3.0%, a sixfold spread on the largest line), the share of extras never invoiced, and the gestoría retainer, which has no source at all and decides whether the product is additive or substitutive spend. The red team is right that the overpayment line is unfalsifiable after the fact, that the register overstates its weight by nearly three (29% of gross failure cost, not 57%), and that the progress-valuation line is not pure arithmetic because "executed" is a human input — it deserves a capture rate nearer 50% than 70%, taking €8,400 to €6,000 and removing a fifth of the reason S2 leads. All three accepted.

The incumbent is not a competitor product. It is a quotation pad, a spreadsheet and a gestoría, costing €3,000–€6,500 a year, of which **86% to 91% survives the purchase**. The product is additive spend justified on value alone. And one decline condition is a property of the product rather than of the prospect: until the Verifactu record layout, QR content, event log and submission channel exist, the incumbent wins on compliance by default in every segment. The honest capture rate on the compliance lines today is **zero**, and every customer-facing claim that says otherwise — claim C9 of the outreach kit, which asserts a persisted tax justification on the invoice the customer sends — must be struck, because the invoice the customer sends is issued by the engine, not by the factory's billing path.

## Economics of tailoring

Measured, not assumed, against this commit. The configuration surface is twenty config keys and seven spec keys, enumerated by composing the resolver's own strict schema — a boundary the code can print, which no competitor in this segment can. Ten config keys carry real structure, one capability (`extraction`) is unreachable because no pack binds its required port, `plugins` is inert because no loader exists, and **the validator proves composability, not correctness**: a tenant resolves and validates with the placeholder tax identifier `B00000000` (check-digit valid, in three tenant files today), with no tax identifier, no fiscal address, an unsubstituted legal-name token, two series sharing an id — and then issues immutable, gapless, hash-chained invoices correctable only by rectification. The cheapest catastrophic failure available is a successful onboarding.

Cost per layer at €70 an hour, one engineer with heavy automated assistance: **L0 8/11/16 hours** by segment (interval 6–26), L1 L0+40, L2 L0+60, L3 L0+120, L3-bridge L0+70, L4 L0+110; L5 is not costed because it is not sold. The result inverts what Phase 5 expected: **tailoring is the smallest term in the model**, 1.5%–2.6% of pre-tailoring CLV and fully covered by onboarding fees already priced (€1,200 / €2,400 / €900, 36%–53% headroom). The risk is year-one support load — 12 to 36 hours per tenant, 44% of revenue in S3 — so the question that protects margin is how much hand-holding a firm needs. Steady-state gross margin is 85.3% / 88.6% / 82.0%, blended 86.5%; net CLV at L0 is €37,615 (S1), €70,646 (S2), €21,580 (S3).

Two investments outrank every Tier C pack and neither was visible from sector adjacency. The **N43 bank-statement adapter** (50 hours, €3,500, payback at two tenants) replaces a bespoke importer that reads one bank's layout; on the artefact's own unsourced estimate, roughly thirty of thirty-seven Tier A+B firms are blocked on that single axis, and each charged the fix as a project is L0+110 hours — **−€2,168, −24.4% in year one**. The **plugin loader** (90 hours, €6,300, payback after four additive requirements) makes the L1 band mean what it says. Pack 1 survives at six-tenant payback; Packs 2 and 3 are declined, needing seven conversions against populations of one to two.

Two concessions, both accepted. The hour figures assume a productivity that cannot be hired: at the artefact's own 3× multiplier for a human-only team, S2's L0 becomes 48 hours and €3,360 against a €2,400 fee, and the fee-positive finding fails. And the layer taxonomy cannot express its own binding constraint — the runtime-tenant and session-isolation work is **280–560 hours, €19,600–€39,200**, absent from every layer row, margin table and payback calculation in Phases 5 to 7. It is in the ask because of that.

## Route to market

**Direct for Tier A, with channel development run in parallel as intelligence-gathering rather than lead generation.** None of the three Catalan channels can deliver a referral inside the sales-cycle window without a reference already existing, and the fastest-moving one — gestorías and asesorías, who hold the books of hundreds of these firms — is also the sharpest competitor, because a conforming billing tool offered through a relationship the client already pays for costs nothing incremental. CAATEEB aparelladors (four to six months) and the Gremi de Constructors, with roughly 1,332 affiliated companies and an existing endorsed-supplier discount programme (six to twelve months), are lagging amplifiers that endorse what they have seen work. CCOC is the wrong body: its members perform about 80% of Catalan public works by volume, which the third hard gate excludes.

Sequencing: **S2 first** — 51% of the near-term pipeline, the shortest payback, and the reference we are trying to manufacture, one of its three Tier A firms chosen on a qualifying call rather than here. S1 second, same jurisdiction pack and same 2027 deadline. S3 last, as a referral source rather than a revenue base. Tier C's installer pack only after the first S2 reference converts.

Qualification is MEDDPICC with two letters rebuilt. The Economic Buyer is usually a duo and both must agree. The Champion function often has nobody to carry the case to, so where no second administrative person exists the test becomes whether the owner will advocate the tool to his own future evening self — and the tell, for Metrics and Champion alike, is whether he can name one completed job whose margin he could not explain afterwards. The Paper Process that moves is the processor agreement. Competition is not a rival ERP but the prospect's own adviser: ask what that adviser has said about 2027, and believe the answer.

Cycle: four to eight weeks with a live trigger, six to twelve without (**assumption, no source**). Six stages, each with an artefact: the trigger opener, the hard-gate qualification record, a demonstration on the prospect's own data, the signed processor agreement, the smoke test and handover note, and the first quarter closed inside the product. Catalan is required — not appreciated — for legally binding documents at a Catalan-first firm, and the Catalan dictionary is machine-authored pending a native reviewer, a disclosure to make before a contract rather than after; whoever runs that call must be a Catalan speaker. The throughput limit is infrastructural, not commercial: **at most two concurrent demo-or-pilot tenants, each on its own stack**, until the isolation work lands.

## Risks and the red team's verdict

The verdict is quoted because the decision above is written against it:

> **"Proceed with conditions.** The asset is real: a printable configuration boundary, a proven separation of jurisdiction from capability, and a segment small enough to enumerate by name. The plan around it is not yet a plan — it prices a product that does not ship the thing it sells, against a list that cannot be phoned, on a number its own sources say is fifteen per cent too high."
>
> "Conditions, all of them gating: run the three experiments before any further outreach, and honour the thresholds. Strike claim C9 from the outreach kit and price compliance at zero until the submission channel exists. Build the N43 adapter before the first pilot, not after the first reference. Close the tenant-identity and session-isolation defects before real customer data arrives, and do not sign an art. 28 security representation until a cross-tenant refusal test passes. Re-derive the revenue band from banded data. Re-extract CNAE and contact channels. Write the profit and loss and the funding ask."
>
> "Fail experiment 1 and stop."

Each of those nine conditions is a numbered yes/no on the one page, in the same order of cost. Where the red team is right, this document concedes rather than argues:

**The load-bearing number is wrong in a known direction.** €134,000 of turnover per employed person is a sector mean inflated by the top 5% and applied to the bottom 95%; the plan's own sourced figures give about **€113,000**. Halve the quotient and S2's captured value falls to 36% of central, value-to-price from 9.7:1 to 3.5:1 — below the ratio on which Phase 6 concedes S3 — and **the whole ICP relocates into the segment the plan tells itself to sell last**. Accepted; decision 7.

**The product sold is not the product that ships.** The effective-dated, self-justifying tax decision is exercised by the CLI and by tests, not by the engine that issues the customer's invoices. Accepted; decision 2 strikes the claim.

**The only dated trigger has no engineering slot.** Phase 6 told Phase 7 to cost the Verifactu build as a dated line; Phase 7 did not. No hours, no owner, 110 days to run. Accepted; decision 5 gives it both at €8,400 (ESTIMATE, the first costing anywhere in this programme).

**Throughput, not demand, binds.** Two pilot slots of ninety days, minus five to ten weeks of platform work by the same hands, is six to eight slots in eighteen months — three or four paying tenants at a blended €4,988, under €20,000 of annual recurring revenue. Accepted; decision 8 states it out loud.

**The ICP has an adverse-selection problem.** The rubric scores a well-run firm _down_, because the product's value is the value of knowing and the highest scorers have chosen for years not to know; and the breach conditions Phase 6 treats as independent are four symptoms of one latent variable, well-run-ness, so the conjunction it computes as improbable is the modal state of the firms scoring best on Cultural distance. The struggling half has the pain and the webmail addresses. The conjunction in the middle passes at roughly 19% of thirty-seven firms — seven, the Tier A cohort, by coincidence. Accepted, and it is why Experiment 1 is a stop condition rather than data-gathering.

Five further gaps this document does not close, listed so they stay visible: no limitation of liability or indemnity in the pilot term sheet, against a €900 fee and a producer-side exposure of €150,000 per financial year per system type; no headcount or bus factor, with one person implicitly the engineer, the Catalan-speaking seller, the support function and the 24-hour incident responder; no named competitors and no price check, when an evening's work decides whether €540 a month is a bargain or triple the going rate; 92% retention and a 12.5-year lifetime assumed for a product that has never renewed a customer, in a cyclical sector; and tenant #1 itself, nineteen of twenty-seven areas still engine-owned, its consent to be named unsought.

## What this run could not do

Only web search reached outside this environment. Every external host answered `403` to the proxy's `CONNECT` — not only `boe.es` and the tax authority but `example.com` too — so the programme was built with no fetch, no download, no API call and no file parse: **no registry pulls** (no SABI, no Informa D&B, no eInforma report, no Registro Mercantil filing, no BORME slice — which is why `directors[]` is empty in 128 of 128 rows and `nif` and `cnae` are null in 64.1%); **no permit datasets** (Open Data BCN's catalogue and the Consorci AOC's per-municipality `llicències urbanístiques` endpoints were located and could not be read, so `permits_last_24m` and `permit_value_total_eur` are empty in every row — the roadmap's own "single best signal of who is actively building" produced nothing); **no direct fetches of primary statistics** (INE table 301 and Idescat's provincial tables were never opened, which is why every provincial count is an ESTIMATE by apportionment, and the XODEL provincial report sat in the bibliography unread); and **no primary legal texts as pages** — Phase 3's instruments were read through domain-restricted search, so identifiers, dates, titles and quoted operative sentences are solid while anything depending on a full annex or table of amounts is labelled UNVERIFIED, fourteen such claims in all.

What one day of SABI and eInforma access changes is the method, not the margin. The work inverts: instead of discovering firms on marketplaces and then resolving them to legal entities, the ICP filter is written as a query — the CNAE set, 8 to 40 employees, €1.0m–€8.0m, S.L. or S.L.U., office in Barcelonès, Baix Llobregat, the two Vallès or Maresme, age 6 to 30 — and the population exports as a **complete frame rather than a 128-row sample**. The 30.8% municipality error disappears because the registered office comes from the register; `revenue_year` arrives attached; the employee series turns "rapid headcount growth" into a measurement; the €113,000-versus-€134,000 question is answered from banded data rather than argued; and Phase 1's sweet-spot bounds become testable by a filter change. Roughly €1,000–€1,500 of eInforma reports puts a verified NIF, CNAE, incorporation date, employee band and last filed revenue behind every row of a hundred-row list — exactly the set QA found least reliable. Ten minutes on an unblocked machine settles INE table 301 and the XODEL report. That is decision 6.

## Falsification

Three conditions, each written before the work starts, each carrying the numeric threshold Phase 11 pre-committed where it gave one, and each meaning **stop** — not "reconsider", not "re-plan".

**1. The list is not a pipeline.** If **fewer than 12 of the 37** Tier A+B firms can be reached by telephone and confirmed as trading renovation contractors, all outreach stops and the programme goes no further than re-extraction; the red team's rule is "fail experiment 1 and stop". Two thresholds from the same experiment re-sequence rather than stop: fewer than **8 of 37** with four or more live sites means the ICP band is wrong rather than the prospects, and more than **26 of those reached** on an unsupported bank puts the N43 adapter ahead of every sales activity.

**2. The value case is not real on customer data.** If the measured price spread on committed purchases is **below 0.5% of third-party spend on both firms** _and_ the unbilled-extras share is **below 15%**, then S2's captured value falls below **€25,000** — a 3.9:1 value-to-price ratio, thinner than the 4.7:1 on which Phase 6 already concedes S3 — and the €540-a-month per-site price is withdrawn and the segment is not sold. A measured gestoría retainer **below €250 a month** for an S2 firm independently invalidates the price ceiling and Phase 6 §4 is rewritten before it is quoted again.

**3. The product cannot be delivered at the measured cost, on the path the customer uses.** If total delivered hours on one real end-to-end onboarding exceed **40** — 2.5× the measured central of 16 — the L0 cost model, the €2,400 fee, the fee-positive finding and the 46–58% year-one margins are all false and no second pilot starts. Independently, if that firm's bank export **cannot be imported without writing code**, or the invoice it would actually issue **does not carry the persisted rule identifier, legal basis and pack version**, compliance is priced at zero in all three segments and outreach halts until the submission channel has hours and an owner. Any one of the three is a stop, not a learning.
