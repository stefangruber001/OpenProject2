01 · Overview

Where the project stands

The system is produced by an ERP factory: a tenant specification is composed at build time from a shared kernel, capability modules, a Spanish jurisdiction pack and a construction/renovation vertical pack. Canei Subirats is company #1. The foundation (Release R0) is complete and continuously verified in CI; the paperless-quotation release (R1) is in progress.

Built & verified — R0

The engine runs

Composable architecture, Spanish VAT engine with persisted legal justification, immutable invoicing with gapless numbering + SHA-256 chaining, supplier price comparison, quote options & versions, chapter catalogue, PostgreSQL with row-level isolation, and a web workspace — all green in CI (~80 tests + live-database + browser end-to-end).

02 · Infrastructure in place

The architecture that is running

Strictly layered, top calls down only. The kernel and capabilities hold zero country- or sector-specific knowledge — a build-time linter fails the pipeline if any tax word or sector term leaks into them. Customisation for a company is data in its tenant file, so a new company is configuration, not a code branch.

03 · Process flows already implemented

What a user can do end-to-end today

A · Quote → Invoice, with the VAT decided by rule

1

Build the quote

Pick a chapter, description, unit; quantity computed from measurement (units × length × width).

2

Client accepts

The accepted version is frozen.

3

Issue invoice

The reduced-rate rule (art. 91.Uno.2.10º) is evaluated from the facts — not chosen from a dropdown.

4

Sealed & justified

Legal basis persisted, gapless number, SHA-256 chain seal. Renders as an es-ES fiscal document.

The rate is a decision with a reason: change the recipient, dwelling age or materials share and the engine switches between reduced (10%) and general (21%) VAT and records exactly why — legally_verified:false until the asesor signs off.

B · Supplier comparison

  • Per-item and per-chapter totals across bidders vs. the initial estimate.
  • Absolute and % variance; a missing price is shown as missing, never as zero.
  • Selecting the winning cost is an explicit choice, never inferred — becomes the quote's cost basis.

C · Quote options & versions

  • Optional lines separate from the base total.
  • Accepted versions frozen; a revision creates a new linked version.

D · Chapter catalogue (catalogue-first)

  • 18-chapter renovation catalogue seeded from the BRD as pack data.
  • Drives the "choose, don't type" quote builder for a non-digital user.
  • English labels now; Spanish keys preserved; Catalan labels ready.

E · Identity & language

  • Canei Subirats brand applied across UI + documents (as data).
  • English working UI with an es/ca switch for handover.
  • Fiscal document always renders in es-ES (Spanish VAT + € formatting).
Verified end-to-end in a real browser: a bathroom renovation quote → acceptance → invoice at reduced VAT with persisted justification and a chained seal.
